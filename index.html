About

Who I am

A brief introduction to my background and what drives me.

I am an ICT Supervisor at the Public Utilities Regulatory Authority (PURA) of The Gambia, where I work in the Directorate of ICT managing server infrastructure, network security, and digital service continuity.

My interest in cybersecurity grew from a Management Information Systems degree that showed me something I keep returning to: an organisation's effectiveness depends on three things working together — people, process, and technology.

I am currently building specialised expertise in cybersecurity, with a particular focus on closing the gap between national policy frameworks and their operational implementation — a challenge that matters for countries like The Gambia.

I am also an ITU Generation Connect Africa Youth Envoy (2024–2030), working to amplify youth voices in digital transformation across West Africa.
