About

Who I am

A brief introduction to my background and what drives me.

I am an ICT Supervisor at the Public Utilities Regulatory Authority (PURA) of The Gambia, where I work in the Directorate of ICT managing server infrastructure, network security, and IT operations for a national regulatory body.

My interest in cybersecurity grew from a Management Information Systems degree that showed me something I keep returning to: an organisation's effectiveness depends on three things working together — the right people, well-designed systems, and the protection of those systems. Security is not separate from how organisations function. It enables it.

I am currently building specialised expertise in cybersecurity, with a particular focus on closing the gap between national policy frameworks and their operational implementation — a challenge I witness firsthand from inside the regulatory ecosystem.

I am also an ITU Generation Connect Africa Youth Envoy (2024–2030), working to amplify youth voices in digital transformation across West Africa.

ITU · Her CyberTracks 2026
Incident Response CyberTrack
Completed

A five-month training and mentoring programme for women in cybersecurity, blending live online sessions with self-paced courses, personalised mentorship and webinars with women leaders in the field. The programme combines a core curriculum shared by all CyberTracks cohorts with a specialised track, and includes an on-site regional training — simulation and hands-on exercises, soft-skills masterclasses, mentorship circles and networking — which I attended in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

The Incident Response track is designed for women working in IT who want to move into cybersecurity, or who are at entry level in CERT/SOC teams.

Track learning outcomes
  • Explain cybersecurity concepts and basic incident response skills in order to contribute to incident response efforts
  • Use entry-level tools and techniques across the incident management lifecycle
  • Understand the influence of AI from an incident response perspective
  • Define key terms in cyber threat intelligence, including threat actors, their motivations and common threats to IT systems
  • Apply digital forensics principles, methodologies and tools at an introductory level
  • Apply soft skills such as communication and teamwork in a professional setting
